Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how SIU AUTO POST handles information when you connect social accounts and prepare scheduled posts.

1. Information used

Depending on the permissions you approve, the app may use account identifiers and display names, authorization tokens, the videos and captions you select, publishing schedules, and posting status or errors. It uses this information to show connected accounts, upload and publish your selected content, and report results.

2. Platform access

SIU AUTO POST requests only the platform permissions needed for the features you choose. A connected platform processes uploaded content under its own privacy policy. The app does not need your social account password; authorization takes place through the platform's own flow.

3. Storage and sharing

Information needed for a pending post may be retained while that post is prepared or scheduled. Account authorization data must be protected and used only to provide the requested publishing functions. Content and account data may be sent to the connected platform to complete an action you request. We do not sell account data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect an account through the relevant platform's security or connected-app settings. To request removal of data associated with SIU AUTO POST, contact the project through GitHub Issues. Do not post access tokens or other secrets in a public issue.

5. Security and changes

We take reasonable steps to restrict access to authorization data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy by posting a revised version here with a new effective date.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project through GitHub Issues without including private credentials.
